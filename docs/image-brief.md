# Photography brief (optional, owner to supply)

The site is designed to look finished without photos: line icons, the product mockup and type carry it.
If you want photography later, keep it in this direction so it stays high-end without looking like a med spa.

**Look:** natural daylight, soft shadows, warm neutral surfaces (linen, stone, light oak), real food with texture.
Muted sage, clay and cream tones to match the palette. Shot from overhead or at 45 degrees. Generous empty space.
**Avoid:** white seamless studio backdrops, gold and marble, spa stones and orchids, measuring tapes, scales,
"before and after" bodies, stock fitness models, neon smoothie bowls.
